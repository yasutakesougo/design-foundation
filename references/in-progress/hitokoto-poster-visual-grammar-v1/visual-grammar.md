# Visual Grammar Candidate

This document materializes the locked candidate from `HITOKOTO-POSTER-VISUAL-GRAMMAR-V1`.

It supplements the existing Accepted Poster Reference. It does not replace or weaken that authority.

## 1. Authority / non-authority boundary

Controlling Accepted Poster Reference:

`references/accepted/hitokoto-poster-canva-output-pilot-v1.md`

This file is an **in-progress candidate** only.

It is not a Foundation, Pattern, Prompt, Skill, Accepted Reference, canonical Review authority, runtime authority, or production authority.

## 2. Controlling information structure

The controlling vocabulary is:

`title → concrete examples → QR → action → safety`

This Accepted wording wins on conflict.

The following terms are aliases only:

- `Hero` ≈ `title`
- `Prompt` ≈ invitation / entry framing adjacent to title
- `Conversation` ≈ `concrete examples`
- `CTA` ≈ `QR + action`
- `Safety` ≈ `safety`

Do not introduce a third normative hierarchy.

## 3. Structure first, presentation lightly irregular

Candidate principle:

`Information = structured`

`Presentation = lightly irregular`

Light irregularity may include small differences in card angle, tape placement, spacing, asymmetric decoration, short marker lines, arrows, or small line-art elements.

Irregularity must not reorder information, obscure reading order, weaken the action path, or create uncertainty about what belongs together.

## 4. Relationship label once; role mapping must remain recoverable

A relationship key such as:

`ひとこと → ひとこと返し`

may be shown once at the section entrance.

Repeated relationship labels on every conversation card should be avoided when the relationship can remain clear without them.

After label-once, speaker / role mapping MUST remain unambiguous.

Acceptable non-label cues include:

- spatial pairing
- sequential adjacency under the once-shown key
- connectors or response arrows
- another equally clear relationship cue

Ambiguous unordered piles, unclear pairing, or unclear first/second speaker roles = **FAIL**.

## 5. Paper-card metaphor is optional

Conversation examples may use a paper-note / bulletin-board metaphor instead of rigid tables or UI cards.

Possible treatments include off-white paper, restrained shadow, small tape accents, subtle texture, and slight visual rotation.

These are optional means, not required authority.

Decoration must not become more prominent than the message.

## 6. Human line-art is atmosphere, not semantic dependency

Simple human line-art may support:

- human presence
- warmth
- eye-flow
- conversational atmosphere

The information structure must still be understandable when the illustration is removed.

Candidate treatment may use single-color simple line-art, limited facial detail, few lines, and restrained expression.

Detailed shading, photorealism, and costume-heavy decoration are outside this candidate direction.

## 7. Accent color is distributed, not dominant

Neutral surfaces should occupy the widest area.

A dark primary tone may anchor hierarchy while pastel accents remain small, distributed, and non-semantic.

Color must not be the only carrier of meaning.

No exact palette or hex value is promoted by this candidate.

## 8. Hand-drawn cues stay peripheral

Hand-drawn character may be used for small atmospheric elements such as:

- short marker underline
- small radiating strokes
- arrows
- tiny doodles
- brief human-voice speech bubbles

Body text, instructions, CTA text, and Safety text must remain readable and neutral enough for their role.

## 9. CTA remains the action endpoint

QR and action text must remain a single understandable action path.

The lively decorative layer must not compete with or obscure the action endpoint.

QR quiet zone and real-device scan reliability belong to Output Preflight, not to this grammar candidate.

## 10. Safety remains visually quiet

Safety information should be grouped in a quieter visual zone.

For Safety:

- decorative handwriting = avoid
- playful tilt = avoid
- color-only meaning = avoid
- readable neutral typography = required

## 11. Exact appearance remains OUT

This candidate does not promote:

- exact V3.4 image
- exact character faces
- exact tape colors
- exact card size
- exact card rotation
- exact decorative leaf shape
- exact marker stroke
- exact QR position
- exact palette
- exact geometry
- exact decoration

Reuse targets structure and role, not a frozen finished picture.

## 12. Promotion remains HOLD

A preferred or successful project-local poster does not become repository authority automatically.

Promotion requires later real operational evidence across materially different cases or repeated variants, followed by a separate Promotion decision process.

Do not manufacture projects solely to create Promotion evidence.

# Review Checklist

Use this checklist for later poster drafts that claim alignment with `HITOKOTO-POSTER-VISUAL-GRAMMAR-V1`.

This checklist is candidate-level only. It is not a Foundation, canonical Review authority, or Human Gate.

Allowed outcomes:

- `PASS`
- `FAIL`
- `HOLD`

If evidence is insufficient, use `HOLD`. Do not invent `PASS`.

## A. Information structure

### A1 — Accepted order preserved

Controlling order:

`title → concrete examples → QR → action → safety`

- PASS: order remains clear and recoverable
- FAIL: decoration or layout reorders or obscures the sequence
- HOLD: full candidate is unavailable or reading order cannot be determined

### A2 — Alias discipline

Aliases may be used internally only:

- Hero ≈ title
- Prompt ≈ invitation / entry framing adjacent to title
- Conversation ≈ concrete examples
- CTA ≈ QR + action
- Safety ≈ safety

- PASS: aliases do not replace the controlling Accepted vocabulary
- FAIL: a third normative hierarchy is introduced or aliases conflict with Accepted wording

## B. Light irregularity

### B1 — Presentation only

- PASS: tilt, tape, arrows, asymmetry, marker lines, or small decoration add atmosphere without changing structure
- FAIL: irregularity creates reading ambiguity, grouping ambiguity, or action ambiguity

## C. Conversation role mapping

### C1 — Relationship label once

A once-shown key such as `ひとこと → ひとこと返し` may establish the relation.

- PASS: relationship is introduced once and does not need repetitive labels on every card
- FAIL: repeated labels become necessary because the layout itself is unclear

### C2 — Speaker / role mapping remains unambiguous

After label-once, each input/reply pair must remain recoverable through one or more clear cues such as:

- spatial pairing
- sequential adjacency under the once-shown key
- connectors / response arrows
- equivalent non-label cue

- PASS: first/second role and pair membership are immediately recoverable
- FAIL: unordered piles, unclear left/right roles, unclear first/second roles, or unclear pair membership
- HOLD: evidence is cropped, incomplete, or too low-resolution to judge

## D. Paper-card metaphor

- PASS: paper-note treatment is used optionally and supports reading
- PASS: no paper-card metaphor is used, while the grammar still works
- FAIL: paper-card treatment is presented as mandatory generic authority
- FAIL: decoration becomes stronger than the content

## E. Human line-art

- PASS: illustration adds warmth / presence / eye-flow only
- PASS: removing the illustration would still leave the information structure understandable
- FAIL: the illustration is required to understand the message or action sequence

## F. Accent and hand-drawn cues

### F1 — Accent color

- PASS: accents stay small-area, distributed, and non-semantic
- FAIL: color alone carries required meaning or overwhelms hierarchy

### F2 — Hand-drawn cues

- PASS: cues remain peripheral and atmospheric
- FAIL: body, instruction, CTA, or Safety readability is weakened by decorative treatment

## G. CTA / QR

- PASS: QR and action text remain a clear final action endpoint
- FAIL: decorative elements compete with or obscure the action path
- HOLD: QR scan reliability is being claimed without Output Preflight evidence

## H. Safety

- PASS: Safety remains visually quiet, neutral, and legible
- FAIL: playful tilt, decorative handwriting, or color-only meaning is used for Safety
- HOLD: actual-size readability cannot be assessed when required

## I. Exact-appearance boundary

- PASS: the draft uses the grammar without claiming exact V3.4 decoration as generic authority
- FAIL: exact faces, palette, tape, card geometry, marker stroke, leaf shape, QR position, or finished image are treated as repository-wide rules

## J. Authority / lifecycle

- PASS: candidate use remains project-local or in-progress unless separately promoted
- FAIL: alignment with this checklist is narrated as Foundation, Accepted, Human Visual Acceptance, Human Output GO, Print/Publish/Deploy authority, or Promotion

## Final verdict

`PASS` requires all applicable mandatory checks to pass.

Any mandatory `FAIL` => overall `FAIL`.

Any unresolved evidence gap that affects a mandatory check => overall `HOLD`.

Checklist PASS alone does not consume or infer any Human Gate.

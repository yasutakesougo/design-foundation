## Selected direction

The user selected the lively handwritten bulletin-board composition built around:

- strong hand-drawn title `日常を、言葉に。`
- broad yellow marker emphasis
- generous but not empty white space
- soft mint / pale blue / pale yellow / cream / peach background accents
- four paired posted-note cards
- slightly irregular card placement rather than a rigid grid
- masking-tape accents
- hand-drawn green arrows between `ひとこと` and `ひとこと返し`
- small meaningful doodles such as lightbulb / eyes / smile / vending-machine cue
- simple adult human illustrations with minimal facial detail and flat restrained color
- small scene-based people rather than unfinished pictograms
- supportive side notes / speech-bubble-like cues
- warm CTA zone with pen cue and QR block
- calm pale-green caution/footer block

## Human illustration direction

People should be simple and approachable, not detailed or realistic.

Required qualities:

- clean outline
- minimal eyes / mouth
- simple hair shapes
- restrained flat fills
- adult proportions
- readable pose and gaze
- scene role is immediately understandable
- not chibi
- not mascot-like
- not unfinished pictogram

Preferred scene roles:

1. upper-left: person noticing / holding a memo
2. right-middle: person considering / reading the posted cards
3. lower-left CTA: two people looking at a memo together

## Why this direction was selected

The selected composition restores the qualities that were missing in earlier Correction-6 / Correction-7 candidates:

- more human warmth
- more visual rhythm
- clearer invitation to participate
- stronger relationship between the four exchanges
- less sterile / web-form-like card layout
- people that support meaning rather than acting as decoration
- a more memorable title and CTA

The target balance remains:

`温かい / 親しみやすい / 人の気配 / 読みやすい / 少しライブ感 / 大人向け / 適度な密度`

Avoid:

`未完成ピクト / 子ども向け / 漫画的すぎる / 文化祭 / 雑貨屋 / 情報過多 / 過密 / 装飾の埋め草`
